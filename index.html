Music Technology Minor

Compose. Record. Experiment.

Union's Music Technology minor brings together music, engineering, computer science, and digital arts. Students learn to compose, record, mix, and build audio technology. It is open to all students, and no musical background is needed.

The minor

Six courses across disciplines

3Required courses
3Electives: one music, one technology, one from either

Technology electives come from Electrical, Computer, and Biomedical Engineering, Computer Science, and Visual Arts. See the official requirements and the academic catalog.

  • Open to all students, with no musical background needed
  • Combines music, engineering, computer science, and digital arts
  • Compose, record, mix, and build audio technology
  • Flexible course options tailored to your interests
  • Hands-on, collaborative work in the studio and the lab
Facilities

Where the work happens

PETER IRVING WOLD CENTER

TARDIS

The Technology, Acoustics, Research, Design and Instructional Studio: a low noise floor recording studio and acoustics lab for recording, mixing, sound design, and research.

Explore TARDIS
TAYLOR MUSIC CENTER

Music Technology Studio

A computer lab for hands-on coursework. Every station has an iMac, a MIDI keyboard, an audio interface, a microphone, and headphones, and the teaching station drives a large display and studio monitors.

Explore the studio
Courses

Where most students start

AMU 140

Transforming Sound, Making Music

An introduction to organizing sound in meaningful ways with computers. Develop critical listening skills, analyze electronic works, and compose original music.

AMU 240

Recording and Mixing Sound

Hands-on work with audio hardware and software, from microphone setup and signal processing to professional recording, mixing, and production.

ECE 102

Introduction to Audio Electronics

Fundamental audio electronics through projects: building basic circuits, working with microcontrollers, and making MIDI controllers.
