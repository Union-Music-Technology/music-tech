Emerson Auditorium

Emerson hosts ensemble concerts, recitals, and visiting artists. Music Technology work-study students run sound and record concerts here, and recordings go to the Music Department archive.

Concert support

How recording requests work

  1. Ensemble concerts go on the work-study calendar early in the term, with their audio and video needs.
  2. About a week before, the assigned student confirms the details with the ensemble director: audio, video, or both; stage layout; sound check time.
  3. On the day, the team sets up and tests everything before doors open.
  4. After the event, recordings are uploaded to the Music Department archive and checked before the cards are cleared.
